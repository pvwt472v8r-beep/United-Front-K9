What We Do

We provide individualized training and support designed to meet each dog’s unique needs. From foundational obedience and behavior improvement to focused scent work and detection training, we create programs that are practical, effective, and centered around long-term success. Our approach is built on structure, patience, and respect, with a strong emphasis on helping dogs and handlers work together with confidence.

Obedience & Control

A well-trained dog is a more confident dog. We help establish structure, reinforce clear expectations, and build reliable obedience that carries over into everyday life. Our training is designed to strengthen communication, improve responsiveness, and create a balanced relationship between you and your dog.

Issue Resolution

Every dog faces challenges, and many behavior issues can be improved with the right guidance. Whether your dog struggles with pulling, reactivity, distraction, anxiety, or inconsistent manners, we work with you to identify the cause and develop a clear, practical plan that supports real progress.

Detection & Scent Work

Scent work is one of the most natural and rewarding activities for dogs. We offer training for detection work, scent development, and nose-driven tasks that engage your dog mentally and physically. Whether you’re building a working dog or simply looking for a focused outlet, scent work is a powerful way to develop drive, discipline, and purpose.

Our Mission

At United Front K9, our mission is to help build better relationships between dogs and the people who love them. Through thoughtful training, clear communication, and practical real-world solutions, we work to improve obedience, confidence, and trust. Whether we are helping a family companion, a working dog, or a future service partner, our goal is to create lasting results that strengthen the bond between handler and dog.
